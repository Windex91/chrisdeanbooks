DARK FANTASY · IN DEVELOPMENT

My Name is Monster.

For thousands of years, he has wandered a dying world, burdened by memories of an age long forgotten and a punishment from which there is no escape.

Across a land scarred by an ancient cataclysm, the remnants of civilisation struggle to survive. Kingdoms have fallen, terrible creatures roam the wilderness, and the colossal beings known as Titans continue to cast their shadows over what remains of the world.

Monster has witnessed it all. He remembers what the world once was, and has seen its rapid decline into the hellish existence it now carries.

But when an unlikely encounter draws him into the company of two mysterious travellers, his solitary existence begins to change. What begins as a journey across a broken continent soon becomes something far greater, forcing him to confront memories he would sooner forget and questions he has spent centuries refusing to answer.

For a creature who has long since abandoned hope, the greatest challenge may not be surviving the horrors of the world.

It may be discovering whether there is still something worth living for.

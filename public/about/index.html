Meet the author.

A little about the person behind the worlds, the characters, and the pages.

HUNTER VALLEY · NEW SOUTH WALES

CHRISTOPHER DEAN

Stories are how we
live a thousand lives.

Christopher Dean is an independent Australian author writing fantasy and speculative fiction. He lives in the Hunter Valley of New South Wales with his family, where imagined worlds have a habit of spilling into everyday life.

His love for fantasy began early, with stories that made the impossible feel within reach. That fascination grew into a passion for creating worlds of his own — places shaped by ancient histories, fierce loyalties and characters confronted by choices with no easy answers.

Outside his writing, Christopher spends time with his family, plays chess, dabbles in woodworking, and competes in Strongman and Highland Games events. Those interests often make their way into his work: the physical weight of a fight, the strategy behind a conflict, and the bonds that hold people together.

His published novels include Beneath Falling Leaves, Wildborn and The Glass Tapestry, with A Dragon’s Tale: The Quest for Dragussy now announced for pre-order, and more worlds in the making.

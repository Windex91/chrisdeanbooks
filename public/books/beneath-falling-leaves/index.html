For ten thousand years, the Veyna have been silent. Now, they speak again.

As King Dorukov turns his gaze westward, the Kingdom of Verdane begins to fracture beneath the weight of old alliances and gathering war. Loyalties strain, ancient hatreds stir, and the cost of ambition grows ever steeper.

Far to the north, among the elves of the Sílnárán, a prophecy long feared has come to pass. An heir is born beneath omens of fire and light — a child the gods themselves have marked.

As whispers of forgotten powers spread and the world edges toward ruin, the fate of kingdoms may rest not on crowns or armies, but on whether light itself will save the world… or burn it to ash.

Beneath Falling Leaves is the opening novel of A Saga of Gods and Kings.

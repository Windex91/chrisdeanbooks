Wildborn

By Christopher Dean

Anya was born to the wilds.

Anya was born to the wilds. Among the Wildborn, survival is earned by patience, blood and steel nerve. One of the tribe's finest hunters, Anya has no intention of setting aside her bow for a safer life.

She saves a charming, infuriating minstrel named Deryn from a pack of wolves, only to discover that he is being hunted by the king of Valemark. His arrival brings the kingdom's cruelty ever closer to her forest home.

Drawn into a world of courts, crowns, betrayal and blood, Anya must find a way to fight for all that has been taken from her. The White Wolf is coming.

✦ THE SMALL PRINT

Shipping & information.

Useful information about orders, deliveries and digital products.

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Business details

Chris Dean Books is operated by Christopher Dean Windle, sole trader. ABN: 26 920 834 116. All prices are in Australian dollars. We are not registered for GST and do not charge GST.

Australian shipping

We offer flat-rate tracked shipping Australia-wide for A$12.95 per physical order, regardless of how many books you purchase. This applies to paperbacks, available signed editions and Special Packs. Orders are carefully packaged and sent using tracked Australia Post services. Orders are subject to available stock. We normally pack and dispatch within three business days of confirmed payment; personalised Special Pack artwork may require extra preparation, and we will contact you if more time is needed. Delivery times vary by destination and postal conditions. Email chris@chrisdeanbooks.com for tracking assistance.

International orders

Readers around the world are welcome. We are preparing tracked international shipping with rates based on destination and packed order weight. For now, physical international orders require an individual quote while we verify protective packaging and destination requirements. Select your delivery country in your book bag to see the available option.

Request an international shipping quote. Please include your country and the books or Special Packs you would like. An enquiry does not place an order or take payment. Orders over 1.5kg, combinations requiring larger packaging, and destinations needing additional customs arrangements require a manual quote. US orders are not available through automated checkout pending customs and prepaid-duty arrangements.

Standard paperback editions may also be purchased through Amazon, subject to availability in your country.

Special Packs and signed books

Special Packs are available for Beneath Falling Leaves and Wildborn. Available signed editions and Special Packs can be included in an international shipping enquiry. We will confirm safe packaging, postage and any required customs arrangements before accepting the order.

Customs and import charges

International customs duties, import taxes and clearance fees may apply. Responsibility and collection arrangements depend on the destination. We will confirm any required prepaid charges before accepting a quoted order; we do not promise that postage includes all import charges. Customs processing can delay delivery.

Digital and mixed orders

Eligible eBooks can be purchased internationally without a physical delivery address or postage charge. A mixed bag is shipped according to its physical items only. The Glass Tapestry eBook is currently exclusive to Kindle Unlimited and is not available for direct purchase here.

EPUB delivery

After payment verification, we email a private EPUB download link to the address supplied at checkout. Links expire seven days after checkout starts. Keep your link private and save your downloaded file; saved files remain usable after the link expires. For replacement access, email chris@chrisdeanbooks.com with your order reference so we can verify the purchase and payment. EPUB compatibility depends on your reader or device; contact us before buying if unsure. Refunded purchases lose access to further downloads.

Returns and cancellations

We do not accept change-of-mind returns after paperback dispatch or ebook delivery. Contact us promptly to request cancellation of an unshipped order. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under Australian Consumer Law, including remedies for faulty, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed goods. Email chris@chrisdeanbooks.com with your order reference and the issue so we can arrange the appropriate remedy. Please agree return arrangements with us before posting anything back.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, order and payment references, and shipping address where needed to process orders, deliver books, provide support and keep business records. Stripe processes payments; our website does not store complete card numbers. Cloudflare hosts our website, order records and private EPUB files. Resend delivers transactional emails. Australia Post receives delivery details needed to deliver your parcel. These providers may process information outside Australia. Buying a book does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

Record retention

We retain essential transaction and business records for five years from the later of record creation and completion of the related transaction, or longer where required by law or an unresolved dispute. We review delivery details after 12 months and remove or anonymise information no longer needed for fulfilment, support, disputes or record-keeping. Retention also applies to relevant order emails and exports we control. Contact chris@chrisdeanbooks.com for access, correction or deletion requests; some information must be retained to meet legal obligations.

Contact

For order, delivery, download or privacy assistance, email chris@chrisdeanbooks.com.

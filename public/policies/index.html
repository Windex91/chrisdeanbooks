Shipping & information.

Useful information about orders, deliveries and digital products.

DRAFT — THIS PAGE REQUIRES YOUR CONFIRMED BUSINESS POLICIES BEFORE CHECKOUT GOES LIVE.

Shipping

Physical books will initially ship to Australian addresses and will be packed and posted by the author. Exact shipping charges, dispatch times, tracking options and carriers will be confirmed and displayed before checkout launches.

Digital editions

Ebooks will be made available by private download once payment is complete. Supported formats, download limits and customer support details will be confirmed before digital orders are enabled.

Returns and refunds

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law will be honoured. A complete change-of-mind and damaged-item policy, together with the return contact details, must be finalised before accepting payments.

Privacy

Stripe will process payment details. To fulfil orders, customer contact and delivery information may be used by Stripe, Cloudflare and the transactional email provider configured for this store. A complete privacy notice, including retention, access and contact details, must be published before launch.

Customer enquiries

A verified contact email address will be provided before sales are enabled.

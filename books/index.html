01 / EPIC FANTASY · A SAGA OF GODS AND KINGS

Beneath Falling Leaves

For ten thousand years, the Veyna have been silent. Now, they speak again.

For ten thousand years, the Veyna have been silent. Now, they speak again.

As King Dorukov turns his gaze westward, the Kingdom of Verdane begins to fracture beneath the weight of old alliances and gathering war. Loyalties strain, ancient hatreds stir, and the cost of ambition grows ever steeper.

Far to the north, among the elves of the Sílnárán, a prophecy long feared has come to pass. An heir is born beneath omens of fire and light — a child the gods themselves have marked.

As whispers of forgotten powers spread and the world edges toward ruin, the fate of kingdoms may rest not on crowns or armies, but on whether light itself will save the world… or burn it to ash.

Beneath Falling Leaves is the opening novel of A Saga of Gods and Kings.

03 / SPECULATIVE FICTION · MULTIVERSE

The Glass Tapestry

One life. Infinite realities. Unimaginable consequences.

Ravi Qadir spent his life keeping a secret: he can see infinite versions of reality layered over his own and take whatever he wants from them. What began as a childhood game became the greatest magic show act on Earth.

But no amount of fame or fortune can quiet his longing for the one thing he cannot take: a life of love and peace he has only glimpsed in another world.

When jealousy and despair drive Ravi to an impossible act, he learns he can do more than take from other worlds: he can cross between them. Trapped in a reality that is not his, he must confront the dangerous consequences of his choices.

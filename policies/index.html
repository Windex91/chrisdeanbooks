Shipping & information.

Useful information about orders, deliveries and digital products.

Orders and prices

This shop sells paperback books and EPUB ebooks directly from independent fantasy author Christopher Dean, trading as Chris Dean Books, based in the Hunter Valley, New South Wales, Australia. All prices and delivery charges are displayed in Australian dollars (AUD) before payment. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not receive customers' full card details.

Paperback stock and delivery

Paperbacks are packed and posted by the author from NSW. We currently ship to Australian addresses only. Available stock is checked when an order is placed; quantities may change as books are reserved or sold. A maximum of five paperbacks may be ordered in one checkout.

Provisional standard Australian postage is A$12.95 for one paperback, A$16.95 for two or three, and A$21.95 for four or five. The postage applicable to your basket is shown during checkout before payment. These charges are subject to final confirmation of parcel dimensions and packaging.

Proposed dispatch commitment for approval: Orders normally dispatch within three to five business days after payment, excluding weekends and NSW public holidays. Transit time starts after posting and varies by destination. If dispatch is delayed, we will contact you at your order email address. Contact us before ordering if you need a book by a particular date.

Ebooks and digital delivery

Ebooks are supplied as EPUB files. After successful payment, a private download link is emailed to the address entered at checkout. Download links expire after seven days for security; contact the author if you need a fresh link. Please check your spam folder if the confirmation does not arrive. Digital purchases have no postage charge.

Purchased ebooks are for the purchaser's personal use and may not be redistributed. This does not limit consumer rights under Australian law.

Returns, refunds and Australian Consumer Law

Your purchase is protected by the Australian Consumer Law. You may be entitled to a repair, replacement, refund or other remedy if a paperback or ebook fails to meet applicable consumer guarantees. These statutory rights cannot be excluded by these store policies.

We do not ordinarily offer change-of-mind refunds or exchanges for books or downloaded ebooks. This does not affect your rights for damaged, faulty, incorrectly supplied or misdescribed products.

If there is a problem, contact chris@chrisdeanbooks.com with your order reference, description of the issue and any helpful photographs. We will assess the problem and explain the remedy available. If a product is confirmed faulty, reasonable return postage costs will be reimbursed where required by law. Do not mail returns without contacting us first so we can provide the appropriate address.

Privacy and customer information

To process purchases and enquiries, we collect details such as your name, email address, books ordered, and an Australian postal address for physical deliveries. Stripe processes card payments; Cloudflare hosts the website, private ebook files and order records; and Resend sends transactional messages. These services may process or store information outside Australia in accordance with their own terms and security practices.

We use purchase information for payments, fulfilment, customer support, fraud prevention, record-keeping and legal obligations. We do not sell customer personal information. Essential browser storage may remember your shopping bag. Records are kept for as long as reasonably needed for order fulfilment, dispute handling and applicable legal or tax obligations, then deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

To ask about your information, request correction, or raise a privacy concern, email chris@chrisdeanbooks.com. We will respond within a reasonable period. This notice should be reviewed when the site adds analytics, marketing lists or new services.

Customer support

For orders, lost links, delivery queries or returns, contact chris@chrisdeanbooks.com. Business: Chris Dean Books (Christopher Dean), Hunter Valley, NSW, Australia. Australian Business Number (ABN) and postal return address will be provided as applicable before store launch.

# Shipping release — 11 November 2026

## Active behaviour

Australian physical orders: AUD 12.95 once per order, tracked Australia Post.
Digital-only: zero shipping and no delivery address. Glass Tapestry EPUB is
blocked for new purchases while Kindle exclusivity is active. Existing paid
order recognition is retained. Paperback prices unchanged. Owner approved pack
prices: Beneath Falling Leaves 35.99; Wildborn 27.99. Both share paperback stock.

International physical orders remain quotation-only: owner confirmed that mailers
have not been measured/test-packed. No automatic international payment or label
is generated. The country selector supplies a book-specific email enquiry.

Country mapping is from Australia Post's actual parcel zones, not geographical
inference: e.g. Brazil and South Africa are Zone 4; Latvia is Zone 5. A mapped
country is not necessarily a serviced or approved destination. Unknown countries,
suspended services, US customs destinations and unapproved routes fail closed.
All international rates are dormant until approvals below are completed.

Server-side packed weight = physical book weights +100g per order +100g/pack.
BFL 500g, Wildborn 240g, Glass Tapestry 330g. No digital weight. >1500g => quote.
Examples: WB 340g; BFL 600g; all three 1170g; BFL pack 700g; WB pack 440g.

## Cost review and activation prerequisites

Published International Standard postage checked 11 November 2026:
NZ 21.60/29/36.40; APAC 26.85/39.40/51.95; US/CA 29.95/43.60/57.40;
Zone4 35.55/49.90/64.20; Zone5 43.80/62.50/81.15 (AUD).
The pricing-update page has an apparent US/CA 1kg typo (34.60); use the
International Standard service page's 43.60 and confirm at label purchase.

Proposed margins BEFORE packaging/payment fees range from 3.15 to 5.85.
Stripe published international card pricing: 3.5% +0.30 of the FULL transaction.
Example NZ Wildborn: 25 -21.60 -((17.99+25)*.035+.30) = approx 1.60 left for
packaging. All three to Zone4: 70 -64.20 -((61.97+70)*.035+.30) = approx 0.88.
A $2 mailer would therefore create a shipping shortfall in both examples.
Other payment methods may cost more. Do not activate without verified actual
packaging costs, payment fee allowance and any sender-paid customs charges.

Before populating SHIPPING_APPROVALS in lib/shipping.js:
1. Test-pack each exact physical SKU/quantity combination, including packs.
2. Record external parcel length/width/height in mm, padding and safe fit;
   confirm AusPost size/girth/weight limits and contents restrictions.
3. Record packaging cost in cents, full transaction fee allowance (bps and
   fixed cents) and destination-specific sender-paid charges.
4. Verify International Standard availability/tracking and customs/tax rules
   per destination, including business registration/collection obligations.
5. Set reviewedUntil and explicitly list approved country codes. Retest and
   deploy. The cost guard rejects an order where the rate cannot cover postage,
   packaging, full transaction payment fees and declared sender customs costs.

Do not remove the hard US/territory block until Australia Post/Zonos requirements
and 10-digit HS codes/country of origin/duty remittance have been confirmed.
Canada has changes to import charge collection from July 2026. NZ has new
customs charges from April 2026; determine whether these affect the chosen
lodgement service and who is charged. UK/EU tax arrangements must be reviewed
for direct sales and mixed-content Special Packs, not assumed from book-only
postage. Complete CN23 with accurate descriptions, value, weight, origin and
classification for each constituent item. Gift wrapping does not make a sale a
customs gift. Check service alerts again before posting; do not assume a date
on a suspension notice means it has resumed.

## Implementation and preservation

Checkout recalculates shipping; browser amounts, regions, weights and approvals
are ignored. Stripe allowed_countries is the single quoted country. Signed
webhook validates country, version, packed weight and shipping total before
stock settlement/emails. Pre-migration AU sessions remain fulfilable.
Shared stock/reservations, promotion codes, payment method selection and private
EPUB access remain intact. No DNS, email records or secret values changed.

New pack Price IDs are versioned in lib/pack-prices.js (not credentials), applied
atomically with the code. Existing Cloudflare price bindings are retained for
historical fulfilment. No Cloudflare binding edits or new secrets are required.
Email template approved in the preceding task is included; not-yet-sent new
orders use it, existing persisted outbox messages remain unchanged.
